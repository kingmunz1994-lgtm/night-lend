How ZK lending works
🔒
Private deposits
Your deposit amount is hidden on Midnight. Only the protocol knows your balance — through a ZK proof, not plaintext.
⊘
ZK health proof
Borrow by proving your health factor exceeds 1.0 without revealing your exact collateral amount.
📈
Earn yield
Interest flows continuously to depositors. Your yield accrues privately. Claim anytime.
⚡
Instant liquidation
If health drops below 1.0, the contract liquidates automatically. No human intervention needed.
